# Visual direction

The storefront takes its structure from a workshop stock sheet: generous square photographs, exact parts columns, clear fitment rows, and two named pickup points. The BMW G82 is the main visual on the landing page, with a separate flat text panel so the car stays visible. Black, stainless-grey, and a small exhaust-heat red accent come from the subject. Barlow Condensed supplies the sign-painted heading character; Barlow handles readable controls and catalog text. Both are loaded locally.

The frontend design skill dealt the **page-is-the-object** direction. Its print-like composition informed the layout. The generic serif and moss palette were replaced with the automotive faces and colours above. The BMW photograph, filters, and part photos take precedence over the sheet's unrelated sample imagery. All animation, sticky motion, transitions, video, and scroll effects were omitted as explicitly requested.

The admin follows the skill's **Achromatic ledger** direction (seed 77771047): a neutral workspace, photographed products, a quiet table, and right-aligned tabular price/quantity columns. The question it answers is: “Which parts are published, and how many are in each storage?” Stock and published counts come from the actual database.

Sample prices, inventory, fitments, locations, and source details are documented in the README and catalog source file. No reviews, customer numbers, or other business claims were invented.
